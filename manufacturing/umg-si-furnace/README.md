# UMG-Si Arc Furnace — Sand to Silicon (experimental R&D)

> Exploring conversion of Bara sand (>98% purity) into solar-grade silicon via the upgraded-metallurgical-grade (UMG-Si) route. **Research-stage:** there is no operational industrial UMG-Si production anywhere today (e.g. Norway's metallurgical-route plant shut in 2023 on economics), and UMG cell efficiency tops out around 20–21% in published work. This is a long-horizon sovereignty goal, not a near-term supply chain.

## Process

1. **Sand extraction** — Bara, North Kordofan (high-purity silica)
2. **Electric Arc Furnace** — 40ft container, 1,400–1,900°C, powered by "Seed Kit" (1,000 PV panels + 2 MWh storage)
3. **Directional Solidification** — Bottom-up cooling, impurities rise to top layer
4. **Plasma Purification** — DS alone doesn't remove Boron and Phosphorus. R&D combines DS + plasma filtration
5. **Top layer removal** — Impure layer cut and recycled
6. **Target**: ~5N–6N silicon at lower energy than the Siemens process (metallurgical routes use less energy than Siemens; exact savings depend on design and are unverified at this scale)

## Self-Replication (aspiration)
The intent is that panels manufactured at one factory help power the next — a bootstrapping goal, subject to real energy-balance validation.

## Status
🔴 **Not started** — Track B R&D, experimental. Requires detailed furnace design and power system specification, and an honest techno-economic study vs. imported polysilicon.
